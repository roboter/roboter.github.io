CircuitFlow Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

CircuitFlow does not collect, share or sell your personal data. Your PCB designs stay on your device.

What stays on your device

CircuitFlow is a PCB layout editor. Everything you create in it is stored locally:

  • Projects you save, as JSON files
  • Custom components and footprints in your personal library
  • Board size, trace clearance and other settings
  • Files you export (SVG, Gerber and GRBL)

We have no servers that receive this information, so we cannot see it.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data about you
  • No selling or sharing of data, because we hold none

Files you save and share

You choose where projects and exports are saved. If you put a file in iCloud Drive, send it with AirDrop, or share it through another app, that service handles it under its own privacy policy. CircuitFlow only accesses files and folders you select.

The browser version

If you use the web version of CircuitFlow, your designs are processed in your browser and are not uploaded to us. The website host may keep ordinary server logs, such as IP addresses and page requests, as most web hosts do.

Children

CircuitFlow collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.
